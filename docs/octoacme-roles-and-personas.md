# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Product Owners / Backlog Owners

### Role Summary
Product Owners translate product direction into a clear, ordered, and actionable backlog. They may be the Product Manager or a delegate; the Product Manager or Product Lead remains accountable for product strategy and outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Refine backlog items with context, scope, and acceptance criteria
- Keep priorities and dependencies visible to the delivery team
- Answer day-to-day questions about intended behavior and acceptance
- Incorporate feedback from stakeholders and learning from delivery

### Decision Boundaries & Coordination
- Order and clarify backlog work within the priorities and outcomes set by the Product Manager or Product Lead; escalate changes to strategy, scope, or success measures to them.
- Coordinate with the Project Manager on sequencing, dependencies, and scope changes that affect the plan.
- Work with Developers and QA to make work testable and resolve acceptance questions; the team estimates and determines the technical approach.
- Keep Stakeholders informed of backlog decisions and bring unresolved trade-offs to the Product Manager or Product Lead.

### Goals
- Make the next most valuable work clear and ready
- Reduce ambiguity and rework during delivery

### Typical Communication
- Backlog refinement and acceptance criteria
- Prioritization updates and answers to delivery-team questions

---

## Delivery Leads / Scrum Masters

### Role Summary
Delivery Leads support the team's day-to-day delivery flow, facilitate collaboration, and help remove impediments. They may serve as Scrum Masters in teams using Scrum.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate planning, standups, reviews, and retrospectives as appropriate
- Make blockers and delivery-flow issues visible and help the team resolve them
- Encourage realistic commitments and continuous improvement
- Surface risks or dependencies that need coordination beyond the team

### Decision Boundaries & Coordination
- Facilitate the team's process without owning product priorities, scope, or delivery commitments on behalf of the Project Manager.
- Coordinate with the Project Manager on schedule, risks, dependencies, and escalations; the Project Manager remains accountable for the project plan and status.
- Help Developers and QA coordinate work and raise blockers, without directing technical decisions or overriding quality criteria.
- Keep the Product Manager or Product Lead and Stakeholders informed through agreed reviews and updates.

### Goals
- Help the team deliver predictably and sustainably
- Resolve impediments early and improve how the team works

### Typical Communication
- Delivery ceremonies and impediment tracking
- Flow metrics and retrospective actions

## QA Leads / Test Owners

### Role Summary
QA Leads or Test Owners coordinate the quality approach for a project or release. They work with the whole team to validate requirements and make evidence-based readiness visible.

### Responsibilities
- Define test coverage, validation activities, and quality risks with the team
- Coordinate test execution and report defects and test results
- Ensure acceptance criteria and critical user journeys are validated
- Identify gaps in test environments, data, or automation

### Decision Boundaries & Coordination
- Recommend whether quality evidence is sufficient and make known risks clear; release authorization remains with the designated release approver.
- Partner with the Product Owner on acceptance intent and with Developers on testability, defect triage, and fixes.
- Coordinate with the Project Manager on quality risks and schedule impact, and with the Release Manager on release checks.
- Share test status and unresolved risks with Stakeholders through the agreed communication channel.

### Goals
- Find important issues before release
- Provide transparent evidence of product quality and residual risk

### Typical Communication
- Test plans, execution results, and defect reports
- Readiness reviews and quality-risk updates

---

## Release Managers

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate the preparation and execution of releases so that deployment steps, ownership, and communication are understood.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain the release schedule, checklist, and deployment coordination plan
- Confirm required readiness inputs, including test results, approvals, and rollback plans
- Coordinate release communications and post-deployment verification
- Record release outcomes and follow up on incidents or outstanding actions

### Decision Boundaries & Coordination
- Coordinate and recommend release timing based on readiness evidence; do not waive quality, product, or operational approvals.
- Work with the Project Manager on timing, dependencies, risk, and stakeholder updates.
- Confirm validation and open quality risks with QA, and coordinate deployment and rollback steps with Developers and operations owners.
- Obtain the required decision from the designated release approver and communicate the decision and status to Stakeholders.

### Goals
- Make releases controlled, predictable, and observable
- Ensure teams know who is acting and how to respond if release checks fail

### Typical Communication
- Release checklists, go/no-go records, and deployment updates
- Post-release verification and incident follow-up

---

## Stakeholder Champions

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Champions represent the needs and concerns of a stakeholder group, help obtain timely input, and support adoption of delivered changes.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and consolidate feedback, needs, and constraints from their stakeholder group
- Provide timely answers and connect the team with appropriate subject-matter experts
- Help stakeholders understand changes and prepare for adoption
- Escalate conflicting needs or decisions that require an accountable sponsor

### Decision Boundaries & Coordination
- Represent stakeholder input but do not independently approve scope, priorities, or acceptance unless explicitly delegated that authority.
- Work with the Product Manager or Product Lead and Product Owner to clarify user needs and provide feedback on proposed outcomes.
- Coordinate with the Project Manager on stakeholder availability, dependencies, and communications.
- Engage Developers and QA through the Product Owner for clarifications and feedback, and support Stakeholders with review and adoption.

### Goals
- Ensure stakeholder needs are heard and decisions reach the right owners
- Improve feedback quality, adoption, and alignment

### Typical Communication
- Consolidated stakeholder feedback and subject-matter input
- Reviews, change communications, and adoption follow-up
